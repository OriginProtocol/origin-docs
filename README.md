# Introduction to Origin

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/Introduction Hero Image (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Origin Protocol builds foundational infrastructure for onchain yield. Its suite of audited, composable smart contracts powers liquid staking, stablecoin, and liquidity management products trusted by protocols, institutions, and individual users alike.

Today, Origin consists of five established products and the [OGN](/broken/pages/7JksLZtkKBvdV3mb235v) token: Origin Ether ([OETH](yield-bearing-tokens/origin-ether-oeth.md)), Super OETH ([superOETHb](yield-bearing-tokens/super-oeth.md)), Origin Dollar, ([OUSD](yield-bearing-tokens/origin-dollar-ousd.md)), Origin Sonic ([OS](yield-bearing-tokens/os.md)), and the Automated Redemption Manager ([ARM](/broken/pages/RxA8pAZazDTAPSL5dtxv)).

Each product is built with a focus on security, liquidity, and composability. Origin’s contracts are fully audited by top security firms and have processed billions of dollars in onchain volume. Our products are widely integrated across DeFi — powering lending markets on Morpho, liquidity on major DEXs, and collateral strategies throughout the ecosystem. This interoperability allows users and protocols alike to access sustainable yield, deep liquidity, and onchain efficiency through Origin’s yield products.

Created by cryptocurrency and fintech veterans, Origin Protocol was founded by serial entrepreneurs, early cryptocurrency investors, and diverse talent that contributed to the likes of YouTube, Google, Dropbox, Paypal, and Coinbase.

The Origin core team is fully doxxed and radically transparent. You can contribute to the development of this decentralized protocol by joining the community in [Discord](https://originprotocol.com/discord).
