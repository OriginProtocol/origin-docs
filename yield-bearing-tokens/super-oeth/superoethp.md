# superOETHp

Origin's second Super OETH launch was superOETHp, which derives Beacon Chain yield from bridged Wrapped [OETH](../oeth/) and rewards from Rooster through a protocol-owned, concentrated liquidity position on Plume.

Origin's four yield-bearing token products ( [OUSD](../ousd.md), [OETH](../oeth/), [Super OETH](./), and [OS](../os/)) share most of the same battle-tested code.
